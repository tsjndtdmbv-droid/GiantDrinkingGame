# Giant Tour de Sip: rules

A bike-factory party board game for 2 to 8 players. The board lives on the TV, your secrets live on your phone.

**Play nice.** For adults of legal drinking age only. A "sip" is one small sip of whatever is in your glass: beer, a soft drink or water all count. Anyone can skip a sip or stop at any time, no questions asked. Never drink and ride (or drive).

## Setup

- Open the game on the TV. It shows a QR code and a 4-letter room code.
- Everyone scans the QR code with their phone, types a name and picks a rider (TCR, Propel, Defy, Revolt, Trance, Reign, Glory or Talon).
- The first player to join is the host. When everyone is in, the host presses **Start**.
- Everybody starts with one secret card. Late arrivals can scan the small code during the game.

## Your turn

- Before your very first roll of the game, take one sip.
- Optional: play a turn card from your hand (they glow when playable).
- Press **Roll** on your phone. Your rider moves that many houses along the road.
- Do what the house says. Mini games happen on the phones, results show on the TV.
- When sips land on someone they get a few seconds to accept, or to play a defence card.

The road has 50 houses in four stages: Propel Road, Revolt Gravel, Trance Trail and Reign Descent. Follow the road and the yellow arrows from **Start** to **Finish**. The first rider to reach Finish wins, makes a toast, and everybody drinks.

## The two card decks

- **Emergency Meeting** (the houses with the meeting table): draw a card from the Emergency Meeting deck. Mostly good news for you, and drinks for your colleagues.
- **Oh No!** (the houses with the pointing finger): draw a card from the Oh No deck. You got caught doing something, and you drink.

The card is shown on the TV and on every phone. The rider who drew it taps **Continue**, then the card happens. A deck is reshuffled when it runs out.

There is no money in this game. Where the printed cards paid a bonus, you now draw a secret card, move forward or roll again. Where they made you pay, you now drink, hand in a card or move back.

### The Cantine

Being sent to the Cantine means you **miss your next turn**. Your rider stays where it is and gets a red "zz" badge on the TV. If you hold a **Skip the Cantine** card it plays itself and you keep riding. A move caused by a card (Kaizen, TPM) never triggers the house you end up on.

## Houses

- **Sip 1 / 2 / 3**: You drink that many sips.
- **Give 2 / 3**: Secretly split the sips between the other riders. Nobody sees until you send.
- **Roll + Sip**: Roll the dice on your phone and drink that many sips.
- **Pick 1**: Three face-down cards. One makes you drink 2, one lets you choose who drinks 2, one gives you another roll.
- **Bell** (Bell Lap): A button appears on every phone. The last rider to tap it drinks 2.
- **R-P-S**: Rock paper scissors. Pick a rival and both choose in secret. Loser drinks 2. A draw means both drink 1.
- **Duel** (Sprint Duel): Pick a rival and both roll. Lowest roll drinks the difference (max 4). A tie means both drink 1.
- **Break** (Breakaway): Pick a partner. Both choose in secret: ride together or attack. Both ride together: everyone else drinks 1. One attacks: the other drinks 3. Both attack: both drink 2.
- **Gear?**: Secretly pick a gear from 1 to 5. Everyone else guesses. Wrong guessers drink 1. You drink 1 for every correct guess.
- **Vote**: A "who is most likely to..." question. Everyone votes in secret. Most votes drinks 2 (ties all drink).
- **Last** (Lanterne Rouge): Whoever has the fewest sips so far drinks 2. A tie is broken at random.
- **All** (Peloton): Everybody drinks 1.
- **Water** (Feed Zone): Everyone has a sip of water.
- **Card**: Draw a secret card. You can hold four; a fifth pushes out your oldest.
- **Rule**: Make a rule (see below).
- **Propel +2 / +3**: Aero boost. Jump forward, then do that house.
- **Flat -3 / -4**: Puncture. Slide back, then do that house.

## Making a rule

From a Rule house or a "make a new rule" card. Your phone offers four ready-made rules, and you can also **write your own house rule**. A rule stays for the rest of the game and applies to everyone, you included. Rules in play are listed on the TV under the board.

Ready-made rules (the game enforces these by itself):

- **Reverse Gear**: Roll a 1 and you move 1 house backwards.
- **Triple Six**: Roll a 6 three times in a row and you go back 12 houses.
- **Round Poll**: After every full round, everyone votes on who drinks a sip.
- **Lead-out**: Roll a 3 and you stay put: pick another rider to move 3 houses forward. Nobody can be pushed onto Finish.
- **High Five**: Roll a 5 and you drink a sip.
- **Slipstream**: Stop on a house where someone already is, and you both drink a sip.
- **Chain Gang**: Roll the same number as the rider before you, and you both drink a sip.
- **Yellow Jersey**: At the start of every round, the rider in front drinks a sip.
- **Tailwind**: Roll a 6 and everybody else drinks a sip.
- **Broom Wagon**: At the start of every round, the rider in last place moves 2 houses forward.

Your own house rules (up to five per game) are shown on the TV with your name. The game cannot check them, so the rider who wrote the rule is the referee. Ideas: "Drink with your left hand", "No first names", "Ring your bell (say ding) before you roll".

## Defence cards (play when sips land on you)

- **Maestro Suspension**: Block every sip aimed at you.
- **U-Turn**: Send the sips straight back to the rider who gave them. Only works on sips from a rider.
- **Pass the Bidon**: Pass your sips to any rider you choose. They can defend too.
- **D-Fuse**: Cut your sips in half, rounded down.

## Turn cards (play on your turn, before rolling)

- **E+ Boost**: Skip the roll and choose your own number from 1 to 6.
- **Bike Swap**: Swap board positions with any rider, then roll as normal.
- **Bike Thief**: Steal a random card from another rider.
- **OverDrive**: The next sips you hand out are doubled.

## Good to know

- No single hit is ever more than 6 sips.
- The opening sip, the finish toast and sips from ready-made rules (High Five, Slipstream, Chain Gang, Yellow Jersey, Tailwind) cannot be blocked with cards.
- If a card sends you to the Cantine and you block the sips with Maestro Suspension, you still go to the Cantine.
- You do not need an exact roll to finish.
- Slow players are auto-played after a timer, so the game never gets stuck. The host can also press **Force skip** in the phone menu.
- If your phone locks or you refresh, just reopen the page. You keep your seat and your cards.
- Players who leave are skipped automatically.
